# Contributing to WRRP

Thank you for your interest in the Wave-Return Resonance Paradigm!

### How to Contribute

1. **Discussions**  
   Feel free to open an issue with questions, suggestions, or critiques.

2. **Improvements to the framework**  
   If you have ideas on how to extend or formalize WRRP (new operators, semantics, examples, physical implementations), please open an issue first to discuss.

3. **Code and pseudocode**  
   Additions to pseudocode examples or transpiler prototypes are welcome.

4. **Documentation**  
   Improvements to README, formal semantics, or examples are highly appreciated.

### Guidelines

- Keep the high-level conceptual nature of the paradigm.
- Maintain clarity and consistency with the core ideas (state_chain, wave-packets, frequency feedback loop, milestone nodes).
- Be respectful and constructive.

We welcome both theoretical contributions and practical implementation ideas.
